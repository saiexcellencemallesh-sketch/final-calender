February

Sant Tulsidas

The Spark of Realization

Tulsidas was once consumed by intense worldly love for his wife, Ratnavali. Legend says he once crossed a flooded river using a corpse as a raft to reach her.

"If you loved the Divine with the same intensity you feel for this body, you would be realized in an instant."

These words from Ratnavali acted as a spiritual shock, redirecting his obsession with the flesh into an eternal hunger for Sri Rama.

March

Goda Devi (Andal)

Andal (8th Century) is the only female among the twelve Alvar saints. Found as a baby under a Tulsi plant in Srivilliputhur, she grew up breathing devotion.

She is celebrated as 'Chudikudutha Sudarkodi'—the bright creeper-like woman who gave the Lord the garland she had first worn herself.

Tiruppavai & Nachiar Tirumozhi

The Tiruppavai is a collection of 30 stanzas sung during Margazhi. Her second work, Nachiar Tirumozhi, expresses her intense longing for Lord Ranganatha.

English Biopic: A cinematic look at her divine life.

Tamil Harikatha: Traditional storytelling of her legend.

Telugu Discourse: Spiritual depth of Tiruppavai.

December

The Saint in YOU

Every Soul is Potentially Divine

As Swami Vivekananda said: "Each soul is potentially divine." You are not different from any of the saints you have met this year.

"The same light that shone through them is the heartbeat within you."

Take a moment to select any quality from these masters—be it Tulsidas's focus, Andal's sovereign claim, or Tyagaraja's musical bliss—and start your own journey today.
