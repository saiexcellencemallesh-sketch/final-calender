January

What is Bhakthi?

Bhakti is the purest form of love that exists between the human soul and the Divine. It is a state of total surrender, a nectar that dissolves the ego until the seeker and the sought become one.

"It is the shortest and sweetest path to self-realization, driven by the overwhelming power of love."

The Inner Stripping

The song Jhoot Chor illustrates the stripping away of falsehoods to find the core of devotion.

The Philosophical Path

Madhusudan Sai explains why Bhakti is the most accessible path to the Truth.

February

Sant Tulsidas

The Story of Transformation

Before his sainthood, Tulsidas was consumed by intense attachment to his wife, Ratnavali. One night, he crossed a flooded river to reach her. Her words changed his life forever:

"If you loved the Divine with the same intensity you feel for this body, you would be realized in an instant."

This spiritual shock redirected his passion into an eternal love for Sri Rama.

Immortal Compositions

March

Goda Devi (Andal)

Andal (8th Century) is the only female among the twelve Alvar saints. Found as a baby under a Tulsi plant, she grew up breathing devotion.

Chudikudutha Sudarkodi

She is celebrated as the one who wore the Lord's garland first. Her second work, Nachiar Tirumozhi, expresses her intense longing and her dream wedding with the Lord.
